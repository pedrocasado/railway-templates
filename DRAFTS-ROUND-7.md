## Topology and deliberate simplifications

- **Three services.** Public `bookwyrm` app, private PostgreSQL, private Redis. The app is public because BookWyrm is registration- and federation-based; a Basic-auth gateway would break ActivityPub and its own cookie login.
- **One Redis, two databases.** Upstream runs separate `redis_activity` and `redis_broker` instances. Here one authenticated Redis serves the activity/session cache on database 0 and the Celery broker/results on database 1 via `REDIS_ACTIVITY_URL` and `REDIS_BROKER_URL`.
- **One volume.** `STATIC_ROOT=/data/static`, `MEDIA_ROOT=/data/images`; exports land under the media root. Upstream's optional Anubis bot challenge and nginx response cache are intentionally omitted.
- **Federation is on by default.** The instance is joinable over its public domain. Operators who want a private catalog must restrict federation from the admin UI after setup.

## Candidates screened out

The marketplace is heavily saturated. Notable exclusions and why:

- **Rename traps.** Jellyseerr returned zero results only because it was rebranded to **Seerr**; `seerr` and `overseerr` listings already exist. Plik and DBHub-style alias collisions were checked the same way.
- **Poor Railway fit despite clean gaps.** Kanidm, Zot and step-ca serve their own TLS and want an operator domain and certificate, which fights Railway's TLS terminator; croc/frp/Chisel are raw-TCP relays; screego depends on WebRTC/TURN; glances monitors only its own resource-capped container.
- **No official image or stale release.** Gollum and Hound are clean gaps but publish no official image and their last releases predate the selected alternatives.
- **Out of demonstrated scope.** The media/*arr and downloader stack (Jackett, Prowlarr, Lidarr, SABnzbd, qBittorrent, Tautulli) are clean gaps but sit outside the catalog's demonstrated positioning.
- **Too heavy for a single draft.** BookWyrm's own upstream compose runs nginx, Anubis, certbot, two Redis services, two Celery roles and a backup job; this draft collapses that to three services and documents what was removed.
